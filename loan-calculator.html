What this estimate covers

A standard reducing-balance loan with monthly payments at a constant rate. It does not model progressive construction interest, changing rates, bank rounding, financing approval or age limits. Monthly interest during construction may differ from the full instalment.

Local/PR progressive transfer-duty bands used: 1% of the first RM100,000, 2% of the next RM400,000, 3% of the next RM500,000 and 4% above RM1 million. Each band is rounded up to the next RM100 for duty calculation. Flat-rate duty also rounds the assessable amount up to RM100.

For foreign non-PR individuals, residential-transfer duty is 8% for instruments executed from 1 January 2026. Other property classifications and exemptions require confirmation. State consent, minimum purchase thresholds and levies vary by state. This tool does not establish purchase eligibility.

New developer purchases under HDA have specific legal-fee scales. Use a solicitor’s quotation; legal fees, taxes and exemptions are not inferred from nationality.

References checked 26 October 2026: Stamp-duty guidance · Solicitors’ Remuneration Order 2023. Estimates are for planning; confirm actual costs with your lender and solicitor.
